    A rangoli board you share with a link

    Everyone with this room's link draws on the same board, live. Each stroke is repeated around the centre, so a single line becomes a ring of petals.

    • Powder, chalk, petals, dots, diyas: pick a brush and a colour, then draw.
    • Invite copies the link. Send it to family and draw together.
    • Lights out darkens the board for everyone, so the diyas glow.

    Clear the board for everyone?

    This erases every stroke on the board for everyone in the room. Anything someone is drawing at this moment will stay.
